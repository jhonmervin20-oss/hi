## Overview
My Demon is a South Korean fantasy romance drama series that aired in **2023**. It follows the story of **Do Do-Hee**, a strong and independent CEO, and **Jung Gu-Won**, a powerful yet arrogant demon who loses his powers after an unexpected connection with her.

To regain his abilities, Gu-Won must stay close to Do-Hee, leading to a series of humorous, emotional, and romantic moments. As they spend more time together, their relationship deepens, revealing the mysterious bond that ties their fates together proving that even a demon can fall in love.
